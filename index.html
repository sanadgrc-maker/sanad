Saudi industrial cybersecurity readiness

Structured, AI-assisted readiness for complex cybersecurity evidence.

Sanad is building a cybersecurity governance and readiness platform for Saudi industrial suppliers, helping teams organize evidence, identify readiness gaps, and prepare for expert review.

Learn more

What Sanad is building

A practical workflow for suppliers that need a clearer, more defensible way to manage cybersecurity readiness without replacing qualified human review.

Evidence organization

Centralizes readiness evidence and maps documents into a structured review workflow.

AI-assisted analysis

Uses simulated AI assistance in the current prototype to surface gaps and support human decision-making.

Expert assurance

Designed around human review, cautious conclusions, and preparation for subsequent authorized assessment.

Prototype V0

Sanad has built a 10-screen clickable desktop prototype demonstrating the journey from onboarding through assessment-package export.

  • Synthetic data only
  • Simulated AI assistance requiring human review
  • No production backend or external integrations
  • No certification or Saudi Aramco approval claims

Current stage

Customer discovery is underway. Supplier outreach has started and the team is testing workflow pain points, internal ownership, readiness needs, and pilot interest.
